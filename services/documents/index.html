      What this printer can do

      Print
      Monochrome laser, automatic two-sided printing, normal/draft/high quality. Documents are converted on the home server.
      Scan
      Colour, grayscale or black and white, from the flatbed or single-sided document feeder. The network scanner advertises 100, 200, 300 and 600 dpi, with PDF and JPEG output.
      Copy
      Standalone copying using the printer’s control panel. Copies print in black and white.
      Paper handling
      250-sheet main tray and manual feed for envelopes or special stock. Use the printer’s paper guidelines; the app currently offers Letter, A4 and Legal on ordinary paper.
      Phone & computer
      AirPrint / Mopria network printing, plus this app through your household sign-in.
      Power saving
      The Brother enters its own sleep mode while remaining available on Wi-Fi. Sending a job wakes it.
