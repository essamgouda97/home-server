# Footage station

## Register

product

## Users

The home-server owner, checking a headless Pi from a phone after a shoot and
organizing DJI footage on a Mac before editing in DaVinci Resolve.

## Product Purpose

Insert a camera card and see a verified server copy without operating a Pi UI.
Track imports, recover interruptions, assign projects and shoots, review Codex
work, and always distinguish copying from verified and safe to remove.

## Design Principles

- Automatic Inbox imports; project selection never blocks a card backup.
- The server is the interface and durable source of status.
- Preserve camera originals and make uncertainty visible.
- Cluster by project, shoot and scene; expose the basis of suggestions.
- Keep the implementation and deployment in the home-server repository.

## Design defaults

Implementation choices: calm, practical, readable. Use a compact responsive
workspace with clear task states. Avoid decorative metrics or marketing copy.
Keyboard access, visible focus, 44px touch controls and WCAG AA contrast are
engineering defaults; no additional personal accessibility needs were supplied.
